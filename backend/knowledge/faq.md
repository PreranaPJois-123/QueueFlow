# Frequently asked questions

## Where can I find my upcoming bookings?
Open **My bookings**. Upcoming appointments and hotel reservations are listed first, with past ones underneath. Select one to see details, the live queue and cancellation options.

## Which specializations are available?
Specializations depend on the hospitals listed on this installation. The Hospitals page lists every specialization that at least one hospital currently offers, and Wayzen can look them up for you.

## What are sample venues?
Venues marked **Sample** are demonstration listings created for this installation. They are not real partner businesses and bookings there are not sent to any real hospital, shop or hotel.

## What can Wayzen do?
Wayzen answers questions using QueueFlow's help articles and the venue information configured here, citing the sources it used. When AI generation is enabled on the server, it can also look up available slots and room prices and prepare a booking for you; nothing is booked until you press **Confirm** on the summary card. Wayzen cannot give medical advice.

## Why does Wayzen say it is in limited mode?
The server has no AI model configured. Wayzen then shows the most relevant help articles instead of writing an answer.
