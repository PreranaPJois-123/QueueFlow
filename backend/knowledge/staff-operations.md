# Operating venue queues (staff guide)

- **Staff > Venue queues** lists only the venues an administrator assigned to you.
- For each provider and day: **Check in** a customer when they arrive, **Start** (or **Start next**) to call the next customer, **Complete** when the visit ends, **No-show** if they do not arrive. Only one visit per provider can be in progress.
- **Report delay** sets how many minutes the queue is running behind; customers are notified once per delay value and their estimates update.
- **Pause** stops the queue from starting visits and stops new bookings for that provider and day.
- Hotel front desk: check guests in on or after the check-in date, check them out, or mark a no-show (which releases the remaining nights).
- Every action is recorded in the audit log.
