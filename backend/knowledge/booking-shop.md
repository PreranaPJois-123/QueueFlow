# Booking a shop service

Shops on QueueFlow include salons, repair centres, consultation desks and other appointment-based businesses.

1. Open **Shops** and search by name, city or category.
2. Open a shop to view its services, their duration and price.
3. Choose a service. If the shop lists staff members, choose one who provides that service, or choose **Any available**.
4. Pick a date and an available time. Times already booked, outside working hours, or too soon to prepare for are not offered.
5. Review and confirm. You receive a booking reference and a queue token.

On the day, **My bookings** shows the token currently being served, your position and an estimated wait. Prices shown are set by the shop; payment happens at the shop, not on QueueFlow.
