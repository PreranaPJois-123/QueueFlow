# Your account, sign-in and privacy

- Create an account with your name, email and a password of at least 10 characters including a letter and a number.
- Your session is kept in a secure cookie and ends after inactivity. **Log out** ends the session on the server; **Log out of all devices** in Profile ends every session.
- Changing your password signs out your other devices.
- **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page sends a reset link if email delivery is configured for this QueueFlow installation. For privacy, the page never says whether an email address is registered.
- Only you can see your bookings and reservations. Staff see the bookings of the venues they operate.
- Wayzen only sees your own bookings, and only when you ask about them.
