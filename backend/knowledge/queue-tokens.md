# Queue tokens, wait estimates and turn alerts

## Checking your token
Open **My bookings** and select the appointment. The page shows your token, the token currently being served, your position and the estimated time until your turn. It updates live; if the live connection drops, it refreshes automatically every 30 seconds.

## How the estimate works
The estimate starts from the visit currently in progress and adds the expected duration of each appointment ahead of you, never earlier than each appointment's scheduled time plus any delay the venue has announced. Durations come from the venue's recent completed visits when enough history exists, otherwise from the configured service duration. It is an estimate, not a guarantee.

## Turn alerts
When your estimated turn is about 20 minutes away, QueueFlow sends one notification: "Your appointment is approaching. Your turn is expected in approximately 20 minutes. Please be ready." You also get a notification when it is your turn, and when the venue reports a delay.

Notifications appear in the bell menu. Optional browser notifications and a sound can be enabled in **Profile > Notifications**; browsers may require you to allow notifications and interact with the page before sound can play.
