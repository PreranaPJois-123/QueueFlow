# Cancellation and rescheduling policies

## Hospital and shop appointments
- You can cancel or reschedule a confirmed appointment from **My bookings** until the venue's cut-off time before the appointment (shown on the booking, typically 2 hours).
- After the cut-off, please contact the venue directly.
- Cancelled appointments immediately free the slot for other customers. Your token is not reused.
- Rescheduling on the same day with the same provider keeps your token; moving to another day or provider gives you a new token.
- If you do not arrive, staff may mark the appointment as missed (no-show).

## Hotel reservations
- Each hotel sets a free-cancellation window, for example 24 hours before the check-in time. The exact deadline is shown on your reservation.
- Cancelling before the deadline is free. Cancelling later records a late-cancellation charge of one night per room, payable to the hotel.
- Once the stay has started, please contact the hotel.
