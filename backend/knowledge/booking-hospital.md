# Booking a hospital appointment

## Steps
1. Open **Hospitals** from the navigation bar. You can search by hospital name or city, and filter by specialization.
2. Open a hospital to see its specializations, consultation services, and the doctors who provide each service.
3. Choose a service (for example a Cardiology consultation). If the hospital lists several doctors for that service you can pick one, or choose **Any available doctor**.
4. Pick a date. Only dates inside the hospital's booking window are shown, and only times that are genuinely free are listed.
5. Select a time slot, add an optional note for the front desk, and review the summary.
6. Press **Confirm booking**. QueueFlow then creates the appointment and shows your booking reference (for example `QF-H-7K3M9PX`) and your queue token (for example `CAR1-004`).

## What you receive
- A **booking reference**, used for support, cancellation and rescheduling.
- A **queue token**, shown at the desk on the day. Tokens are numbered per doctor per day in the order bookings were made.
- An entry under **My bookings**, with live queue status on the day of the visit.

## Important
- An appointment reserves a consultation slot. It does not guarantee immediate treatment; clinics can run late and the estimated wait is updated as the queue moves.
- QueueFlow does not provide medical advice and cannot assess symptoms.
- **In an emergency** (for example chest pain, difficulty breathing, signs of a stroke, severe bleeding or loss of consciousness) do not wait for an appointment: call your local emergency number (112 in India) or go to the nearest emergency department immediately.
