# Reserving a hotel room

1. Open **Hotels** and search by name or city.
2. Open a hotel and enter your check-in date, check-out date and number of guests.
3. QueueFlow lists the room types that fit your party with the number of rooms left for those dates.
4. Each room type shows a server-calculated price: nightly rate x nights x rooms, plus the hotel's taxes.
5. Choose a room type, add any special requests and confirm. You receive a reservation reference such as `QF-R-4HT8K2M`.

## Rules
- Check-in cannot be in the past and check-out must be after check-in. Stays are limited to 30 nights and can be booked up to one year ahead.
- Each room type has a maximum occupancy per room.
- Rooms are held for you only after confirmation. If another guest reserves the last room first, you will be asked to choose different dates or another room type.
- QueueFlow does not collect card details. Payment is made to the hotel directly.

## Changing a reservation
Before the check-in date you can change dates, guests or room type from the reservation page. The change only succeeds if the new stay is available; otherwise your original reservation stays exactly as it was. The price is recalculated.
